# Voice preservation guide

The author speaks in a conversational, teaching tone:

- Keep first-person asides ("let me show you", "notice that").
- Do not flatten lists into dense academic prose.
- Preserve rhetorical questions.
- Fix errors of transcription, not errors of opinion.

**Before (mangled):** "So group O is a way to optimize logic outputs."

**After (cleaned):** "So GRPO is a way to optimize logit outputs."
