# Wikipedia Article Writing

## When to use

Reinforce the trigger and name explicit negatives (teach students: bound I):

- Use when: the user asks to write or draft a Wikipedia-style article on a named topic.
- Do NOT use for: short study summaries, practice questions, quizzes, news blurbs, or promotional biographies.

## Procedure

- [ ] Gather reliable sources about the topic.
- [ ] Identify the standard Wikipedia structure: lead, history, rules/format, notable events, cultural impact, see also, references.
- [ ] Draft a concise lead paragraph summarizing the topic.
- [ ] Write each section with neutral tone, verifiable facts, and citations.
- [ ] Add inline citations using <ref> tags.
- [ ] Ensure the article follows Wikipedia style guidelines (no original research, no promotional language).
- [ ] Review for completeness, neutrality, and formatting.
- [ ] Add a References section listing all cited sources.
- [ ] Add a See also section with related Wikipedia articles.
- [ ] Add appropriate categories at the end of the article.
- [ ] Final proofread for grammar and style.
- [ ] Ensure the article contains at least 5 sections and a minimum of 3 citations.
- [ ] Confirm the article meets Wikipedia's notability and verifiability standards.
- [ ] Save the final article text.
- [ ] End the procedure.
## Done when

The article is a fully formatted Wikipedia entry with a lead paragraph, at least five sections, inline citations, a References list, a See also section, appropriate categories, and adheres to Wikipedia style guidelines.
